# CodeAlpha Task 3 – Data Visualization

## 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard

This project was completed as part of the **CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship – Task 3: Data Visualization**.

The project focuses on transforming raw Superstore sales data into an interactive and visually appealing dashboard using **Tableau**. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, regional performance, category-wise sales and profit, top-performing products, and profit margins.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw data into meaningful visualizations that make business performance easier to understand.

The dashboard helps analyze:

- Sales performance over time
- Regional sales performance
- Category-wise sales
- Category-wise profit
- Top 10 products by sales
- Profit margins by category
- Overall sales and profit
- Total number of orders
- Performance based on different filters

---

## 📁 Dataset

The **Superstore dataset** was independently selected for this project to explore sales, profit, regional performance, category performance, and product-level trends.

### Dataset Details

- **Time Period:** 2021–2024
- **Rows:** 10,194
- **Unique Orders:** 5,111
- **Unique Customers:** 804
- **Categories:** Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology
- **Regions:** West, East, Central, South

The dataset contains information related to orders, customers, products, sales, quantity, discounts, and profit.

> **Note:** Monetary values are displayed in Indian Rupees (₹) for presentation purposes.

## 📊 Dashboard Components

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Total Sales

Displays the overall sales generated during the analyzed period.

### 2. Total Profit

Shows the total profit generated across all orders.

### 3. Profit Margin

Displays the overall profitability of the business.

### 4. Total Orders

Shows the total number of unique orders.

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart showing sales performance over time from 2021 to 2024.

### 6. Sales by Region

Compares sales performance across the four regions:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

### 7. Sales by Category

Compares sales across:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

### 8. Profit by Category

Shows the profit contribution of each product category.

### 9. Top 10 Products by Sales

Highlights the products generating the highest sales.

### 10. Profit Margin by Category

Compares the profitability of different product categories and helps identify categories with strong or weak margins.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to analyze specific portions of the data.

Available filters:

- **Year**
- **Region**
- **Category**
- **Segment**

These filters make it possible to perform more focused analysis and compare different business segments.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The dashboard provides several useful business insights:

### Technology is the strongest category

Technology generates the highest sales and profit among the three product categories.

### West has the highest regional sales

The West region records the highest sales compared with East, Central, and South.

### Furniture has a low profit margin

Furniture generates significant sales but has a considerably lower profit margin compared with Technology and Office Supplies.

### Overall profitability

The dashboard shows an overall profit margin of approximately **12.56%**.

### Top-performing products

The Top 10 Products by Sales visualization identifies the products contributing the most to overall sales.
